THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY

How M&M Laundry Works

From your front door to our high-efficiency commercial wash floor and back — transparent, reliable, and tracked live at every stage.

01

Book Your Doorstep Pickup in 60 Seconds

Select your service, choose a convenient 2-hour pickup slot, and enter your address and security gate code. Customize detergent fragrance (hypoallergenic eco plant-based, clean breeze, or baby-mild) and folding preferences.

02

Secure Courier Handover with 4-Digit PIN

Our verified courier arrives at your door with clean, moisture-sealed bags. Check the courier's photo and vehicle details in your live tracker, verify your secret 4-Digit Handover PIN, and hand over your laundry safely.

03

Laundromat Basket Verification & Dedicated Drum Cycle

At your selected partner laundromat, your basket size or individual items (sneakers, blankets, carpets) are verified. An itemized receipt based on the laundromat's official tariff is sent to your account. We enforce a strict Zero Co-Mingling policy: your garments are washed exclusively in their own sanitized commercial drum.

04

Quality Inspection, Steam Finishing & Crisp Packaging

Every piece undergoes a multi-point quality check for pocket contents, delicate seams, and stain pre-treatment. Dry cleaning and shirts receive high-temperature steam table pressing before protective packaging.

05

Doorstep Delivery with Live Courier Telemetry

Track your delivery driver in real time with GPS telemetry. Your crisp, folded, fragrant laundry is handed over at your door. Pay securely via Instant EFT, Capitec Pay, Card, or mobile POS on arrival.

Legal Policies & Terms of Service

Last Updated & Effective Date: September 11, 2026 · Compliant with POPIA (Act 4 of 2013)

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy (POPIA & GDPR)

M&M Laundry Services (Pty) Ltd ("M&M", "we", "us", or "our") is deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidential personal data of all users across the Republic of South Africa. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in strict compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA No. 4 of 2013), the Consumer Protection Act (CPA No. 68 of 2008), and international standards including GDPR.

2. Information We Collect

When you access our platform, book laundry pickups, register an account, or apply as a driver or partner, we collect:

  • Account & Contact Information: Full name, residential/commercial address, complex/estate name, unit number, security gate access codes, verified email address, and South African mobile phone number (+27).
  • Order & Garment Care Data: Bag count, basket size or individual items (pairs of sneakers, blankets, carpets), detergent selections, water temperature choices, folding specifications, and garment condition annotations.
  • Real-Time Geolocation & Courier Tracking: Precise GPS coordinates during active pickup and return delivery windows to calculate courier ETAs, verify doorstep custody transfers, and optimize route telemetry.
  • Payment & Transaction Details: Tokenized payment credentials, billing postal codes, and payment status via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (PayFast / Peach Payments / Stripe); M&M never stores raw primary credit card numbers on its servers.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, mobile network operators, device identifiers, crash diagnostics, and session logs.

3. Lawful Processing & Purpose Specification

We process collected personal information exclusively for legitimate operational and contractual fulfillment:

  • Dispatching vetted local couriers for doorstep bag collection and return delivery;
  • Routing orders to certified partner laundromats and dry-cleaning facilities;
  • Sending automated transactional SMS notifications regarding courier arrival times and wash stage status;
  • Issuing tax invoices adhering to the South African Value-Added Tax Act (15% VAT);
  • Resolving customer support requests, garment insurance claims, and service disputes.

4. POPIA Data Subject Rights

Under POPIA, all South African data subjects hold the right to:

  • Right to Notification: Be notified when personal information is being collected and processed;
  • Right to Access: Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information and receive a formal record;
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction or deletion of inaccurate, irrelevant, or obsolete personal data;
  • Right to Object: Object at any time on reasonable grounds to the processing of personal information;
  • Complaint to the Information Regulator: Lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator (complaints.IR@justice.gov.za).

To exercise your rights or contact our Information Officer, reach out to privacy@mm-laundry.co.za.

Garment Care Warranty & Protection Standard

1. Standard of Care Protocol

Every order is washed in dedicated, sanitized commercial washers. We never co-mingle laundry from different customers. We adhere strictly to standard wash care tags unless custom customer instructions explicitly supersede manufacturer guidelines.

2. Lost or Damaged Item Reimbursement

While our incident rate is less than 0.02% across all orders, we provide guaranteed reimbursement for verified losses attributable to our cleaning or delivery partners:

  • Claim Submission Window: Claims must be submitted within forty-eight (48) hours of doorstep return delivery, accompanied by photographs and proof of purchase value.
  • Reimbursement Caps: In accordance with industry standards, maximum liability for any individual missing or damaged garment is capped at ten (10) times the individual laundering fee for that item, or up to R1,500.00 per garment (whichever is lower), with a total order reimbursement cap of R3,500.00.
  • Delicate Fabrics & Normal Wear: M&M cannot accept liability for normal fabric degradation, thinning fibers, missing buttons, broken zippers, shrinkage resulting from manufacturer mislabeling, or colors bleeding from non-colorfast fabrics not declared prior to washing.

3. Stain Removal Disclaimer

We apply high-grade, fabric-safe pretreatments to visible stains. However, complete eradication of stubborn or set stains cannot be guaranteed without risking fiber damage.
