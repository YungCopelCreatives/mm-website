How M&M works

From your door to a local laundromat and back, with the status of your order on your phone the whole way.

  1. 1

    Book a pickup

    Choose your service and basket size or number of items, your address, a partner laundromat and a pickup window. Add notes for the driver, like a gate code or detergent preference.

  2. 2

    Pay with Ozow

    You see the full price, including delivery and VAT, before you pay. You are sent to Ozow to pay by instant EFT from your bank. Unpaid bookings are cancelled after an hour and nothing is charged.

  3. 3

    Pickup

    A driver collects your bag in the window you chose. You can see when your order has been picked up on your order page.

  4. 4

    Washing

    The laundromat checks your basket or items, washes your order on its own, and quality checks it before it is packed.

  5. 5

    Delivery with a PIN

    When the driver brings your laundry back, give them the 4-digit PIN shown on your order page. That confirms the order reached the right person.

About M&M

We connect people who need laundry done with verified local laundromats and drivers across South Africa.

Laundry services have often meant unclear prices, lost items and missed pickups. M&M was started to fix that: clear basket and item prices, every order washed on its own, and a status you can check at any time.

Partner laundromats are independent local businesses. We check them before they join and route paid orders to them. Drivers are vetted and paid for every delivery.

Clear prices

Basket and item prices are shown before you book. No scales, no surprises at the door.

Washed separately

Your order is never mixed with another customer's laundry.

Gentle detergents

Fragrance-free hypoallergenic detergent is available at no extra cost.

Questions and answers

How is laundry priced?
Each partner laundromat sets its own prices, and you see them when you choose a laundromat. Everyday laundry is priced by basket size: small, medium, large or family. Sneakers, blankets, carpets and dry cleaning are priced per item. M&M sets the delivery fee and runs promotions. Delivery is and free on orders of or more. You see the total, including VAT, before you pay. See all prices.
Why do I choose a laundromat?
Each partner laundromat is an independent local business. Choosing one near you keeps pickup and delivery quick. See our partners.
How do I pay?
You pay when you book, with Ozow instant EFT from your banking app. Your order is confirmed as soon as the payment clears. If you do not finish paying, the booking is cancelled after an hour and nothing is charged.
What is the handover PIN?
Every order has a 4-digit PIN shown on your order page once you are signed in. Give it to the driver when your clean laundry is returned. Never share it before then.
How long does it take?
Wash & fold is usually back within 24 hours of pickup. Ironing, sneakers, blankets and dry cleaning take 24 to 48 hours, and carpets up to 72 hours.
Can I cancel?
Yes. You can cancel from your order page until the laundromat accepts the order, and you are refunded. After that, please contact us.
What if I live in a complex with a gate?
Add your unit number and gate or intercom instructions in the notes when you book.
What if something is lost or damaged?
Tell us within 48 hours of delivery. Our garment care warranty explains what we cover.
Is my laundry washed with other people's clothes?
No. Every order is washed and dried separately.

Verify your identity in the M&M app

Drivers and laundromat partners are verified before they take orders. Verification happens in the app, with the same account you use here.

  1. 1

    Sign in to the app

    Use the email address or mobile number you applied with. Your website account and app account are the same.

  2. 2

    Upload your documents

    Drivers: ID or passport, driver's licence, vehicle licence disc and proof of address. Laundromats: the owner's ID, company registration, proof of business address and a bank confirmation letter. You can save and come back later.

  3. 3

    Take the face check

    A quick selfie matched to a photo of your ID document, run by our verification partner Persona. It takes a few minutes.

  4. 4

    We review and let you know

    This usually takes 1 to 2 working days. You see your status in the app, and once you are approved you can take orders.

Privacy policy

M&M Laundry Services (Pty) Ltd ("M&M") processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, mobile number and the addresses you save, including unit numbers and access instructions.
  • Order details: services, basket sizes or items, pickup times, notes and order history.
  • Location during deliveries: the assigned driver's location while your order is being collected or delivered, so you can follow it.
  • Payment status: payments are made through Ozow. We receive the payment status and reference; we never see or store your banking login details.
  • Technical data: device and browser information and security signals used to protect accounts and prevent abuse (Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise).
  • Applications and enquiries: what you send us through the driver, partner, business and contact forms.
  • Identity verification (drivers and laundromat partners only): the documents you upload in the M&M app and the selfie taken during the identity check, used to confirm who you are before you can take orders.

Why we use it

  • To collect, wash and deliver your orders through partner laundromats and drivers;
  • To take payment and issue VAT invoices;
  • To send updates about your orders;
  • To answer enquiries and assess driver and partner applications;
  • To handle claims, disputes and fraud prevention.

Who we share it with

Your partner laundromat and driver see the details they need to fulfil your order while it is active. Our service providers (Google Firebase for sign-in, Supabase for data storage, Ozow for payment, and Persona for the identity checks of drivers and laundromat partners) process data on our behalf.

Your rights

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you and get a copy;
  • Ask us to correct or delete it;
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds;
  • Complain to the Information Regulator (complaints.IR@justice.gov.za).

Terms of service

These terms are an agreement between you and M&M Laundry Services (Pty) Ltd. They are subject to the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008. By booking or creating an account you confirm you are at least 18 years old.

The service

M&M is a marketplace connecting customers with independent partner laundromats and drivers.

Bookings, access and cancellations

  • Access: if you live in a complex or estate, give the driver gate or intercom instructions in your booking notes.
  • Cancellations: you can cancel from your order page until the laundromat accepts the order and you are refunded in full.
  • Unpaid bookings: bookings not paid within 60 minutes are cancelled automatically and nothing is charged.
  • Unattended delivery: if you ask us to leave laundry with a concierge or at your door, the risk passes to you once the driver records proof of delivery.

Pockets and existing damage

Please empty all pockets. We are not responsible for cash, keys, documents, jewellery or electronics left in clothing, or for damage caused by items left in pockets.

Prices and VAT

Prices are set by the partner laundromat you choose, per basket (small, medium, large, family) or per item (sneakers, blankets, carpets, dry cleaning), in South African Rand, including 15% VAT. M&M adds the delivery fee and applies any M&M promotion. The full price is shown before you pay.

Disputes

These terms are governed by South African law. Disputes that cannot be settled between us may be referred to the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud.

Garment care warranty

How we care for your clothes

Every order is washed separately and we follow care labels unless you give other instructions.

Lost or damaged items

  • Claims: tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos and proof of value, through the contact form.
  • Limits: we pay up to ten times the cleaning price of the item or R1,500 per garment, whichever is lower, up to R3,500 per order.
  • Not covered: normal wear, loose buttons, broken zips, shrinkage from incorrect labels, or colour running from fabrics that are not colourfast.

Stains

We pre-treat visible stains, but cannot guarantee that old or set stains will come out without damaging the fabric.
